Environment & Errors

Medium gusts: typical unsteady field wind, e.g. open pasture or forest edge on a breezy day.

Reference wind is assumed to be correctly compensated with scope windage. It creates no systematic impact shift. Direction convention: 0° = headwind, +90° = left-to-right. Speed and direction uncertainty create only residual shot-to-shot wind error. The SD values are the standard deviation (1σ) of shot-to-shot fluctuation around the reference wind above — not the reference wind itself.

Target & Range

Typical scope-over-bore offset: ~3.8-4.5 cm (1.5-1.75in) on a bolt gun, ~5-6.5 cm (2-2.5in) on an AR-platform with a raised optic. This matters most at ranges close to your zero.

Bore is solved to a true zero angle at this range (accounting for sight height above), not a straight-line approximation.
